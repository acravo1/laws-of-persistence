# Frequently Asked Questions

## What are the Laws of Persistence?

The Laws of Persistence are a conceptual framework for understanding how individuals, organizations, software systems and intelligent systems preserve capability and continuity over time.

The framework focuses on the preservation of operational capability rather than knowledge alone.

---

## What problem do the Laws of Persistence attempt to solve?

The framework addresses a recurring pattern observed across many domains:

Loss of Context
        ↓
Loss of Continuity
        ↓
Loss of Capability

The objective is to reduce the likelihood of this progression.


---

## Are the Laws of Persistence a methodology?

No.

The Laws of Persistence are principles rather than procedures.

They describe what should be preserved and why.

Organizations may implement those principles through different methodologies, tools and processes.

---

## Are the Laws of Persistence a software framework?

No.

The framework is technology independent.

The principles apply equally to:

- organizations;
- teams;
- infrastructure;
- software;
- documentation;
- artificial intelligence systems.


---

## Are the Laws of Persistence a Knowledge Management framework?

Partially.

Knowledge preservation is one component of the framework.

However, the Laws of Persistence argue that knowledge alone is insufficient.

The ultimate objective is capability preservation.

---

## Why is capability considered more important than knowledge?

Knowledge answers:

"Do we know?"

Capability answers:

"Can we act?"

An organization may possess knowledge while lacking the ability to execute.

The preservation of capability is therefore considered a higher operational objective.


---

## What is the Continuity Hypothesis?

The Continuity Hypothesis proposes that two additional layers should be considered beyond traditional knowledge hierarchies:

Knowledge
        ↓
Wisdom
        ↓
Capability
        ↓
Continuity

Continuity represents the ability to preserve capability through time, change and disruption.


---

## What is the Hidden Knowledge Factory?

The Hidden Knowledge Factory represents knowledge structures that sustain operational capability while remaining absent from formal representations.

Examples include:

- tribal knowledge;
- undocumented procedures;
- historical assumptions;
- expert dependency;
- email-based knowledge;
- chat-based knowledge.

The concept extends the traditional idea of the Hidden Factory.


---

## Are the Laws of Persistence based on existing research?

Yes.

The framework builds upon concepts from:

- Organizational Memory;
- Knowledge Management;
- Systems Thinking;
- Learning Organizations;
- Business Continuity;
- Configuration Management.

The framework seeks to integrate and extend these ideas.


---

## How do the Laws of Persistence differ from Business Continuity?

Business Continuity focuses primarily on maintaining operations during disruption.

The Laws of Persistence focus on preserving the capability required to continue operating.

The emphasis shifts from asset recovery to capability preservation.


---

## How do the Laws of Persistence relate to Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence systems experience continuity challenges similar to those encountered by organizations.

Examples include:

- context loss;
- memory limitations;
- dependency on external knowledge;
- reconstruction of historical state.

The framework can be applied to both human and intelligent systems.


---

## How does PAPE relate to the Laws of Persistence?

PAPE can be viewed as a practical implementation of several persistence principles.

The platform focuses on:

- representation;
- inventory;
- governance;
- recoverability;
- continuity.

Its objective is not merely automation.

Its deeper objective is capability preservation.


---

## What is the most important idea behind the framework?

The most important idea can be summarized as:

The goal is not to preserve knowledge.

The goal is to preserve capability.

The ultimate goal is continuity.
